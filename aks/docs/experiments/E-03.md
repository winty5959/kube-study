## Purpose and expected behavior

Upload 500 generated PNG fixtures while worker replicas start at zero. KEDA must
scale workers for the pending queue and return the Deployment to zero after the
queue drains and its cooldown expires.

## Preconditions

The worker Deployment was at zero. The dev ScaledObject was Active, targeted the
Redis main queue with target length five per worker, and was bounded at two
replicas. The inputs were generated locally and did not contain user photos.

## Procedure and observations

The application accepted 500 generated images. All 500 reached `DONE`. The
worker moved 0→1→2→0; the queue sample peaked at 223.5 items per worker against
the target of five. It scaled to two at 00:50:25 KST and returned to zero at
00:55:56 after the queue drained and cooldown elapsed.

The first new worker emitted a startup-probe connection-refused event before its
listener was ready, then became Ready. No CrashLoopBackOff or OOMKilled state
remained. The full time series and fixture counts are in
[A10 evidence](../../../.omo/evidence/aks-implementation/A10-observability.txt).

## Recovery

The batch cleanup deleted or confirmed absent all 500 fixture rows and objects;
the temporary ID list was removed. The queue and processing list were empty,
worker desired replicas were zero, and the ScaledObject was Active=False.

## Limits

The input was a batch of synthetic compressed PNGs. This verifies queue-driven
scaling and cleanup, not a sustained arrival rate or processing SLA.
