## Purpose and expected behavior

Verify that the database row and original/thumbnail objects survive a Pod
replacement and cluster stop/start, while retaining their PVCs and hashes.

## Procedure and observations

A9 restored a checked Postgres dump and three MinIO objects into AKS PVCs. It
verified the database row and compared object bytes against the restore manifest.
An API lookup returned the restored `DONE` row; the original and two WebP
thumbnails returned successfully. See
[AKS-DATA](AKS-DATA.md) and [A9 evidence](../../../.omo/evidence/aks-implementation/A9-data-restore.txt).

A11 then drained the node holding Postgres, Redis, and MinIO and later stopped
and restarted the AKS cluster. All three PVCs remained Bound; CSI reattached
the disks. The restored sample remained one `DONE` row with a 1,064-byte
original. Its original-object SHA-256 before drain,
`3507bf4bee26d19fc9eb7ff6e98e8063e822e1f5e87a6fc558c115da8452115e`, matched
after drain and stop/start. Both thumbnails and original download passed the
post-recovery smoke. See [A11 evidence](../../../.omo/evidence/aks-implementation/A11-node-and-power.txt).

## Recovery

The dev database, Redis, and MinIO PVCs stayed Bound and retained the restored
sample. Generated smoke fixtures were deleted. The source K3S data and service
were not modified.

## Limits

This checks managed disk persistence for this small restored fixture and an
orderly node drain/AKS stop-start. It does not prove recovery from disk loss,
region loss, or simultaneous multi-node failure.
